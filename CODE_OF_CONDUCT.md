# Code of Conduct

We want this to be a friendly, useful place for people at every experience level, including people who are new to code.

## Our standards
- Be kind and patient. Assume good faith.
- Give feedback on the work, not the person.
- Welcome questions. Nobody has to prove they belong here.
- No harassment, insults, discrimination, sexualised content, or publishing others' private information.

## Scope
This applies in the repository, issues, pull requests, discussions and any space where someone represents the project.

## Enforcement
Maintainers may edit or remove comments, close threads, and block people who break these standards. To report a problem privately, use the repository's "Report a vulnerability or concern" link under the Security tab, or contact a maintainer through their GitHub profile.

Adapted from the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/), version 2.1.
